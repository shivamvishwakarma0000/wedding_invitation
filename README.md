# 🌸 Royal Indian Wedding Invitation | शिवानी संग सौरभ 🌸

An interactive digital Indian wedding invitation website and WhatsApp personalized invite generator.

## 💍 Wedding Details
- **वधु (Bride):** शिवानी विश्वकर्मा
- **वर (Groom):** सौरभ विश्वकर्मा
- **शुभ विवाह तिथि (Date):** शनिवार, 05 दिसम्बर 2026 (Saturday, 05 December 2026)
- **कार्यक्रम स्थल (Venue):** "शगुन लॉन", पापुलर हास्पिटल के बगल में, जंगीरोड, मीरजापुर (उत्तर प्रदेश)

## ✨ Features
1. **Interactive Royal Wedding Invitation (`invite.html`)**:
   - 3D Wine Velvet & Gold envelope opening animation
   - Auspicious Ganesha & Vindhyavasini Devi shlokas
   - "Pull to Reveal" Royal Parchment Scroll with live countdown timer
   - Swipeable 3D timeline for all functions (मतिमंगरा, भत्तवान/हल्दी, शुभ विवाह, विदाई)
   - 1-Tap Google Maps GPS navigation to Shagun Lawn
   - Background Shehnai wedding music with Play/Pause audio toggle
   - Falling rose petals & celebration confetti

2. **Personalized WhatsApp Invite Generator (`index.html`)**:
   - Generates customized digital envelope card photo with guest name & address
   - Formats respectful traditional Hindi WhatsApp invitation message
   - 1-Click WhatsApp direct sender
   - Built-in guest tracker list (saved in local storage)
